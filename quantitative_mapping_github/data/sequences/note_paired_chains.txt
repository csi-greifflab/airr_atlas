The paired chain data (from AlphaSeq and Trastuzumab experiments) were too large to include in this GitHub repository in their entirety. 
For this reason, we provide a subset of the sequences (1,000 rows) for testing purposes.

The paired chains were linked together in the original analysis by concatenating the Heavy and Light chain sequences (VH + VL). 
In the AlphaSeq dataset, the 'Sequence' column contains these concatenated sequences.

Differences between AntiBERTa2 (AB2) and ESM2 embeddings:
- AB2 is an antibody-specific language model, whereas ESM2 is a general protein language model.
- In our analysis, AB2 generally showed higher vicinity scores for antigen-specific clusters compared to ESM2, suggesting a better capture of antibody-specific structural/functional features.
- ESM2 embeddings were typically 1280-dimensional, while AB2 embeddings were 1024-dimensional (depending on the specific version and layer used).
